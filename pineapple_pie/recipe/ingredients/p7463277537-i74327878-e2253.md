## Appropriate Bicycle Behavior
```
**Reminder - Appropriate Bicycling Behavior**

Survivors are expected to use bicycles in a realistic manner. While BMX and Scorcher bikes technically allow players to repeatedly spam the jump mechanic ("bunny hopping") to gain unnatural bursts of speed, this is considered exploiting game mechanics rather than legitimate roleplay.

- Riding normally, performing tricks, and using bikes for transportation is perfectly fine.

-  Repeatedly spamming jump inputs to gain unrealistic speed advantages is considered **powergaming**.

Staff actively monitor abuse of mechanics that create unfair advantages. Using bunny hopping to outrun survivors/zombies, escape situations, or gain unrealistic mobility may result in administrative action, including bans.
```

## Evidence & Item Returns
```
**Reminder - Protect Your Gear: Evidence & Item Returns**

Smart survivors prepare for the unexpected. When opening a ticket, screenshot / video evidence can make the difference between recovering your belongings and having no way to verify what was lost. 

To help protect yourself, all survivors are strongly encouraged to save screenshots or recordings of important information, including:

-  **Vehicle license plates**
-  **SZ storage box numbers and pins**
-  **Base object pins**
-  **Weapon attachments and modifications**
-  **High-value inventory items**

Take periodic screenshots of your inventory, especially when carrying, storing, or transporting valuable items. This is the only insurance you have in the apocalypse! The smartest survivors also keep the last several minutes of gameplay recorded through software such as Medal, Xbox Game Bar, NVIDIA GeForce Experience, OBS, or similar applications. Don't "forget" to record, and be prepared before distaster strikes!

Please also remember:

- Avoid carrying large quantities of zombie ears, rare items, vehicle parts, or other expensive valuables unless you actually need them.

- Saying a weapon was "**fully kitted**" is not enough information for a return request. Many weapons have multiple attachment types and variants. Attachment screenshots or visual proof will be required for accurate restoration of any missing weapon attachments.

- If there is no evidence supporting an item return request, there will often be little or nothing staff can verify.

Help us help you by recording your sessions, documenting important details, and together we can protect your loot!
```

## Leaving The Island & Asset Transfers
```
**Reminder - Leaving The Island & Asset Transfers**

If you choose to delete your character or leave ZOTM permanently, you may **NOT** offload large amounts of items, zombie ears, currency, vehicles, or other valuable assets to other players before leaving.

:no_entry_sign: Dumping wealth, gear, or resources onto friends, factions, or alternate characters is strictly prohibited.

This rule exists to protect the integrity of the server economy and prevent exploitation, wealth funneling, and unfair progression advantages.

Trading and selling assets between active survivors is still allowed when done through legitimate, fair in-character transactions within normal server rules.

However, attempting to bypass progression by transferring significant wealth during departure is not allowed.

:warning: Violations of this rule may result in disciplinary action, including asset removal, character wipes, or account deletion.

Help us keep the wasteland fair, balanced, and enjoyable for everyone.
```
